Ingredients

Mix 500 g flour, 10 g salt, 7 g dry yeast and 350 ml lukewarm water. The dough is sticky, so keep a bowl of water nearby.

Kneading

Knead for ten minutes until the dough stops sticking to your hands, then let it rest for an hour.

Shaping

Fold the dough into a tight ball, place it seam side up in a floured basket and cover it.

Baking

Bake at 240 C for 20 minutes with a pan of water on the rack below, then 20 minutes at 200 C.

Storing

Let the loaf cool for an hour before cutting. It stays fresh for three days in a linen bag.
