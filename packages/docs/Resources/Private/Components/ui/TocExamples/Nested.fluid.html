Delivery

Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day ship the same day. You get a tracking link by email once the parcel leaves the warehouse.

Delivery times

Parcels reach most addresses in Germany within two working days, the rest of Europe takes up to five.

Shipping costs

Shipping is free from 50 EUR. Below that it costs 4.90 EUR within Germany.

Returns

You can return unworn items within 30 days. Print the return label from your order confirmation and drop the parcel at any post office.

Refunds

We refund the price of the items to the payment method you used.

Refund times

A refund arrives within five working days after we receive the parcel.

Contact

Write to support@example.com or call us on weekdays between 9 am and 5 pm.
